How can we help?

Bug, question, or a show that just won't leave your Up Next list — send it over and we'll get back to you.

Before you write in

Most playback and streaming-availability issues come from the streaming service itself, not Late Fee — the app links out to Where to Watch, it doesn't play video directly.

Bug reports

Tell us your iOS version, roughly what you were doing, and what you expected to happen instead. A screenshot helps more than a paragraph.

Feature requests

We read every one of these. Tell us the problem you're trying to solve, not just the feature — sometimes there's already a faster way to get there.

Response time

We're a small team. Expect a reply within a few business days.

Support Ticket

Admit one message. No expiration date.
