What's on the shelf

Built for people who actually rewatch things

Every feature exists because a real watch history needed it — not because a checklist said to add it.

Up Next, sorted right

Home separates what you're actively watching from what's been a while and what you haven't started — with real progress rings, not just a flat list.

Rewatches actually count

Mark an episode watched again and it tracks a real rewatch count — down to the season and the show — instead of quietly overwriting your history.

Zero-wait browsing

Cast, artwork, episode stills, ratings, and where-to-watch links are all cached locally the moment you track a show — no spinner every time you open it.

Upcoming, your way

A real calendar view of what's airing next, switchable between individual episodes and whole seasons, with optional Calendar app reminders.

Favorites & stats that mean something

Shows watched, episodes watched, total time spent — plus Top Watched and Most Rewatched, ranked from your actual history, not a guess.

Your data stays yours

No account, no server, no ads. Everything lives on your device, with optional private iCloud sync across your own devices and a real local backup/restore.
